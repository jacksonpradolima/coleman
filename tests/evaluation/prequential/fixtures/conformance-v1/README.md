These fixtures describe a constructed CI mechanism. They are not historical CI
observations, physical process receipts, independent experimental units or source
admission evidence.

The finite adapter proposes the `full` execution plan (predicted duration 6 s),
then projects to `fast` (2 s) under a 3 s limit. The domain proposal and actual
action differ; deterministic sampling is explicitly recomputed. Original
coefficient-execution fingerprints remain distinct. The decision-time receipt
contains no later outcome. A separate synthetic observation arrives after the
declared build interval, matures, is received and is then applied.

The five canonical wire captures (`receipt`, `observation`, `trace`, `plan`,
`report`) were serialized from consciously reviewed synthetic declarations. They
pin the complete new v1 schema and original DomainRecord omission semantics;
they are not independent numerical oracles. The report retains both full traces
and every derived check, including their distinct explicit recipe bindings.

`hand.json` is independently authored rational arithmetic. It separates common
affine surrogate loss, raw gross modeled seconds, full coefficient L1 and modeled
reconfiguration seconds. It demonstrates a negative net result and a strict
convexity inequality. Neither the reconfiguration charge nor the learner's
regularizer is a measured physical cost or an executed-book fee. Tests calculate
these expectations with Fraction without invoking a COLEMAN optimizer/learner.

Regeneration requires deliberate review of all changed semantics and canonical
bytes. Existing v1 readers are not migrated, and future versions reject.
